# Wavemetrik landing hero: viewport correction

Use the existing logo blue, theme tokens, system typography and original
interactive wireless test-bench illustration. Keep the staggered headline.

The hero must fit laptop viewports, including its specification strip, at
1024x768, 1280x720, 1366x768 and larger sizes. Size typography and drawing by
viewport height; cap growth on tall displays. Short desktop screens down to
600px use compact padding and typography. Do not clip content or force a fixed
height that breaks at zoom.

On phones, keep both actions and the full scene visible in the first 375x667
screen; specifications naturally continue below. At 320px width, allow actions
to wrap and the page to scroll. Preserve 44px control targets and readable labels.

Use concise supporting copy, a compact primary pill and an underlined secondary
action. Keep original illustration behavior, actual product specifications,
link destinations, keyboard access and reduced-motion support.
